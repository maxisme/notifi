Push notifications from anything that can make an HTTP request.

Send notifi.it a title, and optionally a body, image or link, and it appears on your iPhone, iPad or Mac immediately.

https://notifi.it/send?title=hello+world

Encrypted.
Your device holds the only private key. Notifications are encrypted with your public key, so we cannot read them, and deleted from the server once your device has them.

No accounts.
The app makes a send key on first launch. Keys can be renamed and revoked per source.

Urgent alerts.
Mark a key as urgent and its notifications break through Focus.

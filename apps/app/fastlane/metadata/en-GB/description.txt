Push notifications for your scripts, servers, apps or anything that can make an HTTP request.

Send notifi.it a title, plus a body, image or link if you want them along with your unique key. It’s on your iPhone or Mac.

https://notifi.it/send?title=hello+world

Encrypted.
Your device holds the only private key. Notification content is encrypted with your public key at ingest, so we cannot read your notifications. Each one is deleted from the server once your device acknowledges it.

No accounts.
No sign-up, no sign-in, no device linking. The app mints a send key on first launch. Keys can be renamed, paused and revoked per source.

Urgent alerts.
Mark a key as urgent and its notifications break through Focus and stay on the lock screen.
